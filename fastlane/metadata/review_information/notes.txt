ABOUT LOVELYMUSIC
LovelyMusic is a music player application that provides a beautiful,
privacy-focused listening experience. The app plays royalty-free music
from curated collections.

CONTENT LICENSING
All music content in the app is royalty-free and properly licensed:
• Tracks sourced from Pixabay Music (Pixabay Content License)
• Tracks by Kevin MacLeod (Incompetech) under Creative Commons
  Attribution 3.0 License — credited in-app
• Custom artwork created by the development team

No third-party copyrighted content (audio, video, or artwork) is
included in the app, its screenshots, or previews.

GUIDELINE 5.2.3 RESPONSE
The app does not access any third-party streaming service. All audio
content is either bundled within the app or hosted on our own server
infrastructure. No unauthorized content access occurs.

GUIDELINE 5.2.1 RESPONSE
All album artwork displayed in the app, screenshots, and previews
is original artwork created by the development team. No protected
third-party album covers or copyrighted imagery is used.

TESTING INSTRUCTIONS
• No account or login required
• Launch the app to see the home screen with curated music sections
• Tap any song to start playback
• Use the search tab to find songs in the catalog
• Create playlists in the Library tab

SUBSCRIPTION INFORMATION
LovelyMusic offers optional premium subscriptions via StoreKit 2:
• Monthly: $4.99/month (auto-renewable)
• Yearly: $29.99/year (auto-renewable)
• Lifetime: $79.99 (non-consumable, one-time)
Premium features: ad-free, high quality audio, unlimited skips,
synced lyrics, equalizer.
To test: Settings > Premium section. All IAP products configured
in StoreKit sandbox.

PRIVACY
All user data stored locally on-device. No personal data transmitted
to third-party servers. No IDFA or tracking frameworks used.
