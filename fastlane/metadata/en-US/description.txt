LovelyMusic is a beautifully crafted offline music player featuring
a hand-picked library of CC BY 3.0 instrumental tracks composed
by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) and licensed under Creative
Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0).

WHAT'S INSIDE
A curated catalog of 20+ cinematic, ambient, and uplifting tracks
bundled directly inside the app. No account. No sign-up. No
external streaming. Open the app and everything is ready to play.

PLAY OFFLINE, ANYWHERE
All music is stored on your device and plays without an internet
connection — perfect for flights, commutes, and focused work.

YOUR LISTENING, YOUR WAY
Build custom playlists, save your favorites, and revisit your
recent plays. Everything stays on your device — nothing is uploaded
to any server.

SING ALONG (WHEN AVAILABLE)
Optional synced lyrics pulled from the public LrcLib community API
for tracks that have them. Lyrics highlight in real time as the
music plays.

DRIVE WITH MUSIC
Full CarPlay support — browse the bundled library and play
hands-free from your car's dashboard.

DESIGNED FOR YOU
A clean, minimalist interface with smooth animations, thoughtful
typography, and native Dark Mode. No clutter — just the music.

OPTIONAL PREMIUM
Premium is entirely optional and unlocks quality-of-life features
on top of the bundled library: uninterrupted listening, enhanced audio
quality, an equalizer, unlimited skips, and refined UI themes.
Every track is included by default — Premium only enhances the experience.

MUSIC CREDITS
All music by Kevin MacLeod — incompetech.com — licensed under
CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Full attribution, source URLs, and per-track license evidence are
shown inside the app under Settings → About → Music Credits.
